Clean Tone Studio

A channel strip for recording clean electric guitar: smooth and detailed, full-bodied, with plenty of sustain, a clean reverb and a smooth delay. Voiced for both single-coil and humbucker pickups.

  • Free
  • Open source (AGPLv3)
  • VST3 + standalone ASIO app
  • Windows 64-bit
Download for Windows Source code on GitHub Get the -win64.zip from the latest release.

What it does

Put it on a mono guitar track: it outputs stereo, so the delay and reverb spread around a centered guitar.

Input

  • Pickup: a corrective voicing for Single Coil (more body, softer ice-pick) or Humbucker (less mud, more detail), or Flat.
  • Hum Filter notches 50 or 60 Hz mains hum; a smooth Noise Gate keeps hiss down at high sustain.

Sustain / Dynamics

  • A smooth optical-style compressor: notes ring longer without a jump in volume.
  • Pick brings each note's attack forward or softens it; Blend mixes in the dry signal; LOOKAHEAD catches pick attacks early.

Tone & Cabinet

  • Warmth: a tube-style stage, mostly even harmonics, with a bias that breathes with your picking. 4× oversampled; 0 % is completely clean.
  • Bass, Mid, Presence, Treble, Low and High Cut, and Clarity, which trims low-mid mud only when it builds up.
  • Cabinet: Open 1x12, Classic 2x12 or Closed 4x12, or load your own impulse responses (two slots with a blend).

Delay

  • Repeats that get darker as they fade; time changes glide like tape instead of clicking.
  • Sine or Tape modulation, Ducking, Ping-Pong, and sync to your DAW tempo (or Tap tempo in the app).

Reverb

  • Early reflections and a dense, smooth tail without metallic ringing.
  • Ducking lets it bloom when you stop playing; a Low Cut keeps the low end clear.

Auto Level

Keeps the output as loud as the input whenever you change presets or settings, so you can compare tones without louder sounding better. It depends only on the settings, so the same settings always sound the same.

Play through the standalone app

The same sound, controls and presets without a DAW: plug in your guitar and play.

  1. Install your sound card's ASIO driver, open the app and choose Options → Audio/MIDI Settings…
  2. Set the device type to ASIO, pick your card, turn on the input your guitar is plugged into and choose your headphone or monitor outputs.
  3. Set the buffer to 64–128 samples for low latency (raise it if you hear crackles).
  4. Untick Mute audio input. The app remembers your device and settings next time.

Install the plugin

  1. Download CleanToneStudio-x.y.z-win64.zip from the latest release and unzip it.
  2. Double-click Install VST3.bat and click Yes when Windows asks for permission. (Or copy the Clean Tone Studio.vst3 folder into C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3.)
  3. Rescan plugins in your DAW and put Clean Tone Studio on your guitar track.

Details

SystemWindows, 64-bit. Any VST3 host; the app supports ASIO and Windows audio.
PresetsStudio Clean, Glassy Single Coil, Warm Jazz Humbucker, Humbucker Chime, Funk Snap, Endless Sustain, Ambient Swell and DI Clean (No Cab), plus your own.
Gain stagingSet your interface so the guitar peaks around −12 dBFS; that's the level Auto Level and the compressor are tuned for.
