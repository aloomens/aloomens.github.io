Open Mastering Studio

A complete mastering chain in one plugin, with an assistant that listens to your track and sets everything up for a clean, clear, loud and still dynamic master.

  • Free
  • Open source (AGPLv3)
  • VST3 + standalone app
  • Windows 64-bit
Download for Windows Source code on GitHub Get the -win64.zip from the latest release.

The chain

Seven modules in mastering order. Click one to edit it; each switches on and off without a click. The chart above the controls follows the module you select.

Input → EQ → Multiband → Glue Comp → Saturation → Stereo → Limiter → Output + Dither

EQ

Low and high cut, two shelves and three bells, on stereo, mid or side. Drag the points on the live analyzer.

Multiband

Three bands, each with its own compressor. Optional linear-phase crossovers and program-dependent release.

Glue Comp

A stereo-linked bus compressor with a soft knee, a sidechain low cut and a parallel mix.

Saturation

Tape, Tube and Soft Clip, plus modeled Tape (magnetic hysteresis) and modeled Tube. 8× oversampled, so it stays clean on bright material.

Stereo

Width in three bands, Mono Bass for a tight low end, and Mono-Safe so widening never pushes the correlation below zero.

Limiter

A look-ahead, true-peak limiter (Classic or Transparent style) that holds your ceiling, usually −1 dBTP for streaming.

Output and dither

Final trim and TPDF dither for 16 or 24-bit, with psychoacoustic noise shaping.

Meters

EBU R128 / BS.1770 loudness: integrated, short-term, momentary, loudness range and true peak, plus gain reduction and correlation.

Master Assistant

A starting point in seconds, which you then shape by ear.

How it works

  • Play your mix (or press LEARN in the plugin while the song plays).
  • Choose a target: a genre curve (Pop, Rock, Hip-Hop, R&B, EDM, Singer-Songwriter, Acoustic, Jazz, Classical) or your own reference tracks.
  • Pick a style and a loudness, then press Analyze & Apply.

What it sets

  • EQ moves toward the target's tonal balance.
  • Glue, multiband, saturation and width only where your mix needs them.
  • Limiter gain to reach the loudness you chose, with a −1 dBTP ceiling, checked by rendering the result.
  • Revert puts your previous settings back.

Comparing honestly

Level-matched Compare

Hear the unprocessed mix at the same loudness as the master, time-aligned, so you judge tone rather than volume.

A / B slots

Two complete sets of settings to switch between, with Copy A>B.

Standalone app

Open a WAV, AIFF, FLAC, MP3 or OGG file, shape the master while it plays, then Export Master… to WAV or FLAC.

Install

  1. Download OpenMasteringStudio-x.y.z-win64.zip from the latest release and unzip it.
  2. Double-click Install VST3.bat and click Yes when Windows asks for permission. (Or copy the Open Mastering Studio.vst3 folder into C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3.)
  3. Rescan plugins in your DAW and insert Open Mastering Studio last on your stereo master bus.
  4. The standalone app (Open Mastering Studio.exe) runs from anywhere: no install needed.

Details

SystemWindows, 64-bit. Any VST3 host; the app supports ASIO and Windows audio.
LatencyAbout 7.5 ms, reported to your DAW, which compensates automatically. Each linear-phase option adds about 90 ms.
Sample rates44.1 to 192 kHz.
PresetsDefault, Streaming Ready (−14), Loud & Punchy (−9), Warm Analog, Wide & Airy, Bass Control, Gentle Glue and Podcast / Voice (−16), plus your own.
First startWindows SmartScreen may warn that the app is from an unknown publisher (it isn't code-signed): click More info → Run anyway.
MeasurementsDistortion, aliasing, true peak, latency and CPU are in MEASUREMENTS.md in the download.
